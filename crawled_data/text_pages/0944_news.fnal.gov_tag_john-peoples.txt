John Peoples, Fermilab director at time of top quark discovery, dies
Chicago Tribune, July 27, 2025
director
John Peoples
Physicist John Peoples Jr. was the third-ever director of Fermilab, and in his 10 years in charge oversaw efforts to boost the power of the Tevatron, a circular particle accelerator that in 1995 contributed to the discovery of the top quark, the largest of all observed elementary particles.
Fermilab mourns the passing of John Peoples, third director
June 30, 2025
John Peoples
lab director
Tevatron
Press release
John Peoples passed away on June 25, 2025. He was the third director of Fermilab, remembered as a prolific physicist and a hands-on leader.
