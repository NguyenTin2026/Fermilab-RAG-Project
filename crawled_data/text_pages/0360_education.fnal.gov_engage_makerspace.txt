MakerSpace
Join us to get hands-on with STEM! Our MakerSpace provides learners with an opportunity to explore STEM fields through making, experimenting, and play. MakerSpace programs encourage students to ask questions, get creative, and develop the 21st-century skills needed to join the next generation of STEM professionals.
MakerSpace programs
MakerSpace Open Lab
Welcome Makers! Join us for Open Lab to enjoy hands-on STEM activities. All ages are welcome, and activities are designed for elementary age students.
Learn more
STEM Spotlight
Students ages 8-10 work with their guardians to discover STEM careers and get hands-on with Fermilab science.
Learn more
Visualizing STEM
Students ages 11-14 spend four days getting hands-on with physics through MakerSpace projects inspired by science.
Learn more
Teacher Studio
Teacher Studio is a hybrid learning community for formal and informal educators interested in hands-on, maker-based learning. The Teacher Studio is a collaboration between the University of Wisconsin-Madison PLACE, Building for Kids children’s museum, Madison Public Library, and the Fermilab Office of Education and Public Engagement.
