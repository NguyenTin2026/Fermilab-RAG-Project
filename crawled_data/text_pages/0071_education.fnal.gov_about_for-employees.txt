For Fermilab employees
A variety of opportunities are available for lab staff and users interested in participating in education and public engagement programs
Fermilab employees and users who share their time to support our programs are critical to EPE success! If you are interested in helping with any of our established EPE programs or have new ideas, please contact
edreg@fnal.gov
.

Time-charging codes
Fermilab staff are able to charge time for their work on preapproved EPE programs and events. A form to request approval for activities are located
here
. All employees must submit a request in advance of programs or events and must receive approval before charging their time.
