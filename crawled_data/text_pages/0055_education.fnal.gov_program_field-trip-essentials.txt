Field Trip Essentials
Field Trip Essentials prepare teachers for their field trip to Fermilab!
Bring your students to Fermilab for NGSS-aligned field trips that connect to the authentic science being done at the laboratory.
This program gives you all the essential concepts, content, logistics, and several engaging classroom activities to prepare your students for the physical or life science field trip. Teachers must participate in a training session with our staff before scheduling a visit for your students.
Session details
Registration is required at least one week prior. You must have a REAL ID-compliant document to enter Fermilab. Visit https://get-connected.fnal.gov/real-id-at-fermilab/ for more information.
Location:
Lederman Science Center
Time:
4:15pm-6:00 p.m., with a 10-minute break
Fee:
$30
Registration and payment are required 1 week prior to the event.
Refunds for cancellation must be requested at least one week prior to the event,
A minimum of 7 participants is needed to run the program. Participants will be notified of the program status 1 week prior.
