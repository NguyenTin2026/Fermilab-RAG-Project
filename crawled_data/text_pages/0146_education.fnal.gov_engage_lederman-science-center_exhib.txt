Exhibits
Our exhibits are fun and helpful tools for exploring the world of very small objects, such as particles.
Lederman Science Center’s interactive exhibits are available to the public to explore Fermilab’s science.
The indoor exhibits are conceptually arranged in four exhibit rooms. These rooms tell the story of Fermilab science — what the lab studies, how the lab works, and what tools and methods the lab uses. Outdoor exhibits provide space for learners to explore physical and life science through play to deepen their learning.
