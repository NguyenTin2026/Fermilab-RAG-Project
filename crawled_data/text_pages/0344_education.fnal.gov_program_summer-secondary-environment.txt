Summer Secondary Environmental Science Institute
These Next Generation Science Standards-aligned activities will enhance your classroom experience and connect you to the authentic ecological science of Fermilab, with support from expert Fermilab ecologists.
About the teacher workshop
This five-day course, offered at Fermilab, will deepen your understanding of soil composition and formation, ecology and restoration, aquatic ecology, and climate change through field and laboratory activities. It is appropriate for a high school level environmental science or AP environmental science class. Please dress appropriately as the workshop will be outside for a portion of each day.
