City of Chicago recognizes Illinois Tech Nobel Laureate Leon Lederman with honorary street designation
November 23, 2020
Illinois Institute of Technology
Leon Lederman
From the Illinois Institute of Technology, Nov. 23, 2020: The Illinois Institute of Technology has announced that it will designate a portion of 33rd Street that crosses through Mies Campus as Honorary Leon Lederman Way. The proposal was put forth by faculty members in the Department of Physics in honor of Nobel Prize-winning physicist and longtime Illinois Tech faculty member Leon Lederman, who died in 2018.

$1.9 milion DOE grant will support training of accelerator scientists
December 4, 2019
accelerator science
accelerator technology
education
Illinois
Illinois Institute of Technology
Northern Illinois University
DOE has awarded a $1.9 million grant to Northern Illinois University and the Illinois Institute of Technology for the training of next-generation workers in accelerator science and technology. The program will cover student tuition costs for two years and fund paid research assistantships at Fermilab and Argonne. Physics professors Michael Syphers and Philippe Piot, both experts in particle accelerator research and technology, are leading the effort at NIU.
