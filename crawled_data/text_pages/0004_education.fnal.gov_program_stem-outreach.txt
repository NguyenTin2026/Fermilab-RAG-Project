Programs
STEM outreach
Ideal for school fairs and STEM-focused community events, our STEM outreach programs highlight Fermilab science through hands-on activities and exciting demos or connecting with staff experts from around the lab.
Available presentations
Fermilab Expert Speaker-
Request a Fermilab expert to share their work with your audience. Presentations are typically 1 hour and include experts in fields such as physics, engineering, quantum, and more.
Available hands-on experiences
Our STEM Outreach Kits are designed for large scale Open House or STEM Fair style events. Each STEM Outreach Kit is facilitated by Fermilab Expert staff and include multiple hands-on activities and demos for your event.
Intro to Fermilab Science
– Explore different areas of Fermilab science, such as: fundamental elementary particles – the basic building blocks of matter, neutrino beam production and oscillations, particle accelerators, quantum science, dark matter and dark energy and more.
Fermilab Careers
– Explore the Fermilab community by learning more about the many diverse jobs at the lab. Learn about the tools and skills used by scientists, engineers, operators, and more at the lab.
Electricity and Magnetism
– Explore how electrical currents and magnets interact and affect each other with the activities provided in this kit. Using them you will learn how to create magnetic fields from electrical currents and vice versa, what role magnets play in particle accelerators, and the basics of electrical circuits.
Light and Color
– Explore the behavior of light and how it interacts with different substances with the activities provided in this kit. Using them you will learn about reflection and refraction, how materials react to different colors of light, and about how to identify chemicals by the light they emit.
Quantum Physics
– Explore the unique and unexpected behavior of subatomic particles with the activities provided in this kit. Learn about how pairs of particles can remain connected even over great distances, and how particles can be in multiple configurations simultaneously until it is observed.
This kit is designed for grades 6 and up.
Neutrino Physics
– Explore the mysterious behavior and properties of neutrinos with the activities provided in this kit. Learn about how Fermilab produces, detects, and studies neutrinos; and about how neutrinos are able to oscillate to change their type.
This kit is designed for grades 6 and up.
