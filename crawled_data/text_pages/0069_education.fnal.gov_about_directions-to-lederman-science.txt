Plan your visit
Fermilab’s Batavia site is open to the public.
Click here
for more details on hours, activities, and other site access requirements.
Visitors 18 years of age and older will be required to show a REAL ID-compliant document to enter Fermilab. Please see the appropriate documents
here
.
Visiting Lederman Science Center
Lederman Science Center Hours
Monday-Friday 9 a.m.-5 p.m., Closed on Saturdays and Sundays
The Lederman Science Center will be closed June 19th in observation of the holiday. The Lederman Science Center will also be closed on July 3rd in observance of the holiday.
The Lederman Science Center will also be closed for lab holidays. You can view the
public calendar here
.
The center is easily accessible from Fermilab’s main entrance at Kirk Road and Pine Street in Batavia, just three miles north of the I-88/Farnsworth Avenue tollway interchange.
From the main entrance, turn east into the Fermilab site. Lederman Science Center is the second building on the right.
