Fermilab ecological science
Fermilab has a long history of land management based on ecological science and stewardship values that enhance the environment. The laboratory site contains most of the major types of ecosystems representative of the United States Midwest and offers unparalleled opportunities for environmental studies and volunteer activities.
In 1989, Fermilab was designated a National Environmental Research Park, one of six across the United States.
